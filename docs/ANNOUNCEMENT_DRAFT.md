# Public announcement draft — source-first map update

Active Matter // Field Operations is a free, independent fan guide. The **Source Atlas** is now the primary map entry: 13 opt-in, community-hosted overhead raster reference areas (including Dalniy Island regional overview), with zoom, pan and named raid-variant source links. **Gigastructure** is under **Special / In progress** for user-supplied floor screenshots, not a fictional finished flat map.

The viewer deliberately keeps **all 275 historic candidate markers hidden** over source rasters and local screenshots until independently reconciled. Exits, timed extracts, anomalous and dynamic points vary by patch, spawn and mode. Regional source-tile windows are not verified exact raid boundaries, and heights are not certified.

The legacy offline 2.1.1 companion remains available with PvE Solo/Team, PvP planning, dossiers, investigation tracking, music and personal backups. Its older geometric maps are illustrative and should not be relied upon for exact positions; this update does not silently replace that ZIP. Tiles are fetched from the third-party source only after explicit user consent.
