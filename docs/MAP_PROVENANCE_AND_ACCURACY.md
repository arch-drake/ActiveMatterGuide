## 2.1 isometric projection disclosure

The new isometric viewer transforms the existing 2D map plane by rotation and vertical compression. It is not 3D, game geometry, or topographic survey; no new verified roads, building height, water, vegetation, ground cover, or marker positions were obtained. Mouse-to-map inverse projection was added to preserve the intended coordinate of personal map pins. Use supplied screenshots and at least three unchanged landmarks to align overlays for practical use.
