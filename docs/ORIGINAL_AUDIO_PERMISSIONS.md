The six included MP3 soundscapes were synthesized for this field guide using generated waveforms and noise, without samples, recordings, melody transcriptions or material taken from Active Matter or third-party music libraries.

They are provided **royalty-free** for personal use, editing and sharing with this guide. They are not official game music. If used in monetized media or publicly published works, confirm any platform's policies and your legal rights.

Fracture Signal and Shelter Afterglow are retained from the earlier self-generated edition; Perimeter Drift, Anomaly Choir, Midnight Extraction, and Signal Interference are newly synthesized for this update.

No external links or background audio streaming are required. Playback begins only after the user clicks Play.

## 2.1 additional original tracks

Dead Relay, Monolith Dream, Red Zone, Glass Reactor and Dawn After Extraction were generated with deterministic procedural oscillator and noise synthesis and encoded as MP3. No third-party sample, game soundtrack, or copied melody was used. Their standalone source generator is included at `source/generate_original_music.py`.
